What is our mission?

Our mission is to provide users with no sign-in necessary, an space where they can find any recipe they want offering a complete database for everyone wanting to learn simple recipes, or embark in the deep woods of cooking. We expect this webpage to act as a tool for people to use in their everyday basis and as a source to share opinions on liked and disliked recipes for all to be constant participants of the development of the app.
