ABOUT OUR WEBSITE

Chef It Up is an accessible resource for finding recipes you can tailor to your dietary restrictions, serving size, and other preferences, filters you apply as you search, with no account and no setup. Our mission is to give anyone a place to find recipes that fit their needs, whether they're learning a few simple meals or taking on something more ambitious. Every visitor can also rate and review recipes, so the community's opinions help shape which recipes rise to the top.

Our preliminary idea for our dataset is TheMealDB database.

  1. Search and browse. Every recipe appears as a card with a photo, rating, prep time, difficulty, dietary tags, and meal type, so you can tell at a glance whether it's worth a visit.
  2. Fit to need. Narrow by prep time, meal type, or dietary requirement. Active filters stay visible so you can adjust or clear them as you go.
  3. Rate and discuss. Leave a star rating and a review under any name you like, no account needed. Reply to other reviews to ask a question, agree, or push back.
  4. Save and shop. Save your favorite recipes for future reference, or add any necessary ingredients to a quickly generated shopping list for ease of use.
